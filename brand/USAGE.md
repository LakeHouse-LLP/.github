# Brand usage rules

## Do

- Use light logos on dark backgrounds and dark logos on light backgrounds (`<picture>` + `prefers-color-scheme`).
- Keep clear space around the mark roughly equal to the cap-height of the wordmark.
- Use colors from `palette.json`.
- Point public links at the custom **`domain`** from `.lakehouse/org.json` (never `*.github.io`).
- Credit the brand as **`brand`** from `org.json` (stable across org renames).

## Do not

- Recolor the logo outside the palette without Sen approval.
- Stretch, rotate, or add drop shadows / glow.
- Place the logo on busy photography without a solid scrim.
- Use client marks, Ennead marks, or third-party logos in this folder.
- Hardcode the GitHub **org login** into brand CDN paths; org renames must not break brand identity.

## Social preview

- Canvas **1280×640**.
- Include brand mark + short product name; no screenshots of client work.
- Upload per repo in GitHub settings (Sen) or via documented automation later.

## Questions

Sen decides exceptions. Track brand tasks in SenZhang-Todo when needed.
